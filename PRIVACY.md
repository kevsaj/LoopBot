# LoopBot privacy policy and terms

The privacy policy and terms of use live on the KIS Studios site, and the Google Play listing and the game's Settings link to them:

- Privacy policy: https://www.kisstudios.net/loopbot/privacy
- Terms of use: https://www.kisstudios.net/loopbot/terms

In short: LoopBot collects no personal data. No accounts, ads or third-party tracking; progress is saved only on your device. The Android app can send anonymous gameplay statistics (players can turn them off in Settings) and in-game feedback to a Google Sheet KIS Studios owns; see scripts/telemetry/README.md.

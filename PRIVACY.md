# LoopBot privacy policy

_Last updated: November 7, 2026_

LoopBot is a puzzle game by KIS Studios. This policy covers the LoopBot app on Android and the LoopBot web demo.

## What we collect

Nothing. LoopBot does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information or usage data.

- **No accounts.** You never sign in or give us your name, email or age.
- **No ads, no analytics, no tracking.** The game contains no advertising or analytics code and makes no network requests while you play.
- **No permissions.** The app does not use your camera, microphone, location, contacts or files.

## What stays on your device

Your progress (floors cleared, stars, hints) and the programs you build are saved only on your device, in the app's own storage. If Android backup is on, Android may include this game data in your personal device backup with Google, under Google's own policies. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

## Children

LoopBot is made for players aged 13 and up and is not directed at children under 13. Because the game collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
